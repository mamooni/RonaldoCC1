Common questions

When does a download unlock?

The moment the payment clears. Files stay in your account for 12 months and re-downloading is always free.

What does the licence cover?

Each listing states its own licence — single team, unlimited projects, or extended. The extended tier is the only one that covers reselling a finished product.

Can I get a refund?

Every listing shows its refund window before checkout: 14 days for bundles, 7 for single assets, and some items are marked not eligible. Open a ticket and support reviews it by hand.

An order was cancelled — why?

Orders left unpaid for 48 hours are cancelled automatically. Nothing is charged for a cancelled order.

What are the ZIP codes on the goods page?

Each creator is listed with the postal code of their studio, so you can filter by region. Codes marked with a tick were confirmed against a public postcode database.

How do I add funds?

The wallet takes crypto only — USDT on TRC-20, credited automatically after three confirmations. See the deposit page for the current address and fee.
